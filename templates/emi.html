How EMI Is Calculated

EMI depends on the financed amount, annual interest rate and repayment period. A larger down payment reduces the financed amount, while a longer repayment period usually reduces monthly EMI but can increase total interest. This calculator provides an estimate only. Actual loan terms depend on the bank or financial institution.

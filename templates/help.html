FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions

Find quick answers to common KisanVision360+ questions.

Open the Crop Advisor from the sidebar. Enter your farm information such as soil, location, season and available water. The system can then provide crop recommendations based on the available information.
Open Disease Detection and upload a clear crop or leaf image. The trained CNN model analyzes the image and returns the predicted disease class when the image can be processed successfully.
Open Market from the sidebar to view available agricultural market-price information. Use the information to compare prices before making selling decisions.
Farmers can open Marketplace, add an agricultural product, enter its name, category, price, quantity and location, and then manage the listing and received customer orders.
Open Settings and use the central language option. The selected language is stored in the application session and is used throughout the application.
Open Profile or Settings from the sidebar to manage your account information and application preferences.
